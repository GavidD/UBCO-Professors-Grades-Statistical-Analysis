How to read the table
  • Diff = the professor's average minus everyone else's average in the same courses, in percentage points, weighted by how many of the professor's students were in each course. Positive = their sections average higher than colleagues teaching those same courses.
  • Prof avg / Others avg use the same course mix and weights, so they subtract cleanly.
  • Median course diff and % courses above show whether the gap is consistent or driven by one course.
  • 95% CI is a bootstrap interval for Diff (resampling courses). Clear gap = the interval excludes 0. In the chart, the bar is Diff and the thin line is the CI.
  • Click any row to see the course-by-course breakdown.
Caveats
  • With hundreds of professors, a handful will show a "clear gap" by chance alone. Treat the extremes as leads, not verdicts.
  • Averages also reflect class size, term, course mix, and which students enrol; they are not a measure of teaching quality.
  • Co-taught sections count for each listed instructor. Older terms may also list TAs, which the thresholds mostly filter out.
